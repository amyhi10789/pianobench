Prompt 4F: D4 played three times

[Insert the shared instructions above.]

Press and release D4 three separate times. After each press, the finger must
lift and D4 must visibly return to its normal raised position before being
pressed again. Produce three short, clearly separated D4 sounds. The three
events must be distinct presses and distinct sounds rather than one sustained
note. Do not hold D4 continuously, and do not press or sound any other key.
