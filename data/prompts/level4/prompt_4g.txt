Prompt 4G: A4 played four times

[Insert the shared instructions above.]

Press and release A4 four separate times. After each press, the finger must
lift and A4 must visibly return to its normal raised position before being
pressed again. Produce four short, clearly separated A4 sounds. The four
events must be distinct presses and distinct sounds rather than one sustained
note. Do not hold A4 continuously, and do not press or sound any other key.
