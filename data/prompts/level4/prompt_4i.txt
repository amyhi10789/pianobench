Prompt 4I: F4 played three times

[Insert the shared instructions above.]

Press and release F4 three separate times. After each press, the finger must
lift and F4 must visibly return to its normal raised position before being
pressed again. Produce three short, clearly separated F4 sounds. The three
events must be distinct presses and distinct sounds rather than one sustained
note. Do not hold F4 continuously, and do not press or sound any other key.
