Prompt 4A: C4 played three times

[Insert the shared instructions above.]

Press and release C4 three separate times. After each press, the finger must 
lift and C4 must visibly return to its normal raised position before being 
pressed again. Produce three short, clearly separated C4 sounds. The three 
events must be distinct presses and distinct sounds rather than one sustained 
note. Do not hold C4 continuously, and do not press or sound any other key.
