Prompt 5E: Two contrasting three-note chords

[Insert the shared instructions above.]

Perform exactly two separate three-note chord events. First, press C4, E4, and 
G4 simultaneously using three different fingers of the same right hand. 
Release all three keys completely and lift the fingers clearly away from the 
keyboard before beginning the second chord. Then press D4, F4, and A4 
simultaneously using three different fingers of the same right hand.
Within each chord, all three keys must begin moving downward at the same 
moment and all three audible pitches must begin simultaneously. Produce 
exactly C4, E4, and G4 in the first chord, followed by exactly D4, F4, and 
A4 in the second chord. Do not arpeggiate either chord, overlap the two chord 
events, or press or sound any additional keys.
